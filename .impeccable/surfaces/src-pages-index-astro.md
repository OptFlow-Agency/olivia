# Surface brief: Olivia home page

Mode: Persuade. Audience: prospective diners, phone-first. Actions: read the menu, find hours/location, enquire about private events. Content is sample and labelled; no reservations flow; no invented reviews/awards.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Olivia is a mid-century European hotel dining-room identity programme: one grotesque and one didone, rules and leader dots, each band a different printed ground. Refuses the cream-paper-and-terracotta restaurant template and the dark-photo-hero-with-reserve-button template.
OWN-WORLD: The user's six-colour palette is the whole world. Slate #455A58 drenches the opening, stone #C5C5BC is the carte paper, walnut #65492C holds events, espresso #28170C holds gallery and footer; tan #7F6648 and taupe #8C867E are rules and ornament only. Bodoni Moda display, Hanken Grotesk text, hairline rules, dot leaders, a plate drawn in concentric rings with an olive sprig.
STORY: The visitor sees a calm, expensive room in seconds, reads the real-looking carte, trusts the practical facts (hours, address, phone), and sends a private-event enquiry.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slate field. Left 45%: h1 "A modern European table, set slowly.", short deck, filled paper "View the menu" and outlined "Find us". Right: an oversized plate seen from above (concentric stone/tan/walnut rings, espresso well, drawn olive sprig) bleeding off the right and bottom edge. Sticky wordmark nav on top; hours/address/phone strip hairline-ruled at the fold.
FORM: Hotel dining-room identity programme, my own candidate 4 of 7 (also considered: printed carte on walnut, candlelit room, ceramic glaze studio, cellar archive, swatch book, tasting-course sequence). Seed key a251600f.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
