# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Prospective diners researching a modern European fine-dining restaurant, mostly on a phone before deciding where to go. They want to judge the cuisine and room, read the menu, check hours and location, and consider the space for a private event.

## Product Purpose
Public website for Olivia, a modern European fine-dining restaurant. It lets visitors browse the menu, find the location and opening hours, see the space in a gallery, and enquire about private events. Success is a visitor who walks in, or sends an event enquiry, having already formed a clear impression of the food and the room.

## Positioning
Modern European fine dining, presented as a restaurant with a specific room and kitchen rather than a generic template. The specific mechanism (chef, sourcing, signature dishes) is undecided and must come from the restaurant.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- In scope: menu browsing, location and opening hours, private events enquiry, gallery of the space.
- Not selected: online table reservation. Do not build a booking flow; a phone or email contact line is enough unless the user adds it later.
- Content is placeholder for now: menu, address, hours, contact details and dish names are sample content and must be clearly marked for replacement.
- The six photos in the palette reference image are mood references for the colour palette, not confirmed brand assets.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Olivia.
- Palette (user-supplied, binding): #C5C5BC, #8C867E, #7F6648, #455A58, #65492C, #28170C. Warm stone, taupe, tan, deep teal-slate, walnut brown and near-black espresso.
- The user wants the site to feel fancy and appealing.

## Evidence on Hand
No real menu, address, hours, photos, reviews, awards or press exist yet. Future work must not fabricate testimonials, press mentions, awards, chef biographies presented as fact, or review ratings.

## Product Principles
- The menu and the room are the proof; lead with them rather than claims.
- Every practical fact (hours, address, contact) is reachable in one step on a phone.
- Placeholder content is honest and easy to find and replace.
- Restraint reads as expensive: fewer, better-composed elements over decoration.

## Stack
Astro (existing scaffold in the project, `astro@^7`), static output.
